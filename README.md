### 🎤 **AIMusicBot Features:**
AIMusicBot is an awesome Telegram bot that lets you play music from various platforms like YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, and more! 🎧

---

### 📜 **Key Commands:**

- `/play <song name>` – Play a song from YouTube, Spotify, etc. 🎶
- `/pause` – Pause the current track 🛑
- `/skip` – Skip to the next track ⏩
- `/stop` – Stop the music 🎵
- `/queue` – View the song queue 📝
